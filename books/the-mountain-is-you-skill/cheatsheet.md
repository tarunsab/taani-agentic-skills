# Cheatsheet

## Diagnose the pattern

| If you notice… | Ask… | Then… |
|---|---|---|
| Repeated self-defeat | What need does this meet right now? | Replace the function, not only the behavior. |
| Resistance to something good | Is it wrong, or merely unfamiliar? | Clarify the goal and acclimate gradually. |
| A fresh start every time it gets hard | What discomfort am I fleeing? | Check the pattern, then stay and resolve what is workable. |
| Perfectionism | What am I avoiding by not beginning? | Produce a modest first version and improve it. |
| Constant busyness | What would I feel if I became still? | Choose the top five tasks; simplify and release the rest. |
| A goal that leaves you empty | Do I actually want this, or its image? | Reassess the inherited success story. |

## Read the emotion

- **Anger** → boundary, injustice, or energy for constructive action.
- **Sadness** → loss that needs to move in waves.
- **Guilt** → repair actual harm, or investigate an inherited burden story.
- **Jealousy** → a desire you may not be allowing yourself to pursue.
- **Resentment** → an expectation of who someone “should” be; return to what you can learn or choose.
- **Regret** → information about what to create or prioritize now.
- **Chronic fear** → a control strategy; identify the legitimate need and the part within influence.

## Instinct or fear?

| Present instinct | Fear / intrusive thought |
|---|---|
| Quiet, calm, and clarifying | Loud, urgent, and panicked |
| Responds to what is happening now | Projects into an imagined future |
| Opens options or shows a response | Assumes intent, predicts, or catastrophizes |
| Arrives briefly and can be released | Repeats and creates a spiral |

## When triggered

1. What happened, specifically?
2. What am I feeling in my body?
3. What need, value, boundary, or loss is underneath it?
4. What part is evidence and what part is projection?
5. What safe release is available now or later?
6. What is the smallest responsible course correction?

## Build the new baseline

- Choose one microshift, not a total reinvention.
- Repeat it until it is familiar.
- Write the principle it serves: “When X, I do Y because it protects Z.”
- Plan for plausible problems; do not rehearse every unlikely one.
- Ask for help where you lack expertise.
- Let people dislike a responsible choice without making their approval the goal.

## Return to inner peace

When trying to force happiness, step back toward neutral. Allow the actual feeling, arrive in the present, and notice a small joy without requiring life to be perfect. Schedule unfilled time, play, learning, and nourishing relationships; enjoyment is part of the life being built, not a reward after the mountain is gone.
