## Core Frameworks

1. **Lifespan versus healthspan.** The objective is not merely more years but more years of cognitive, physical, and emotional capacity.
2. **The Four Horsemen.** Cardiovascular disease, cancer, neurodegenerative disease, and type 2 diabetes/metabolic dysfunction develop through long, overlapping trajectories.
3. **Medicine 3.0.** Prevent early, individualize trial evidence, reason explicitly about risk and the risk of doing nothing, look over decades, and treat healthspan as an outcome.
4. **Objective → strategy → tactics.** Name the future capabilities worth protecting, design a strategy against likely risks, then use measurable and revisable tactics.
5. **Exercise as the anchor.** Train aerobic efficiency, VO2 max, strength, and stability; use the Centenarian Decathlon to define real-life capacity.
6. **Nutrition 3.0.** Replace diet ideology with nutritional biochemistry, energy and protein sufficiency, metabolic feedback, and sustainability.
7. **Sleep and emotional health.** Sleep is a biological requirement; emotional regulation and relationships are part of longevity, not extras.
