# Glossary

Definitions below are concise, book-grounded study aids; “Ch” refers to the chapter note.

**AMPK** — Cellular low-energy sensor that promotes fuel use, mitochondrial adaptation, and autophagy while inhibiting mTOR. (Ch 5)

**Anabolic resistance** — The age-related tendency for muscle to respond less strongly to a given protein or training stimulus. (Ch 15)

**ApoB** — Protein marker representing the number of atherogenic lipoprotein particles, including LDL, VLDL, IDL, and Lp(a). (Ch 7)

**APOE e4** — APOE variant associated with higher Alzheimer’s risk but not a deterministic outcome; effects vary by context. (Ch 9)

**Atherosclerosis** — Long-developing arterial plaque process in which apoB particles enter the vessel wall and can trigger inflammation and thrombosis. (Ch 7)

**Autophagy** — Cellular recycling and clearance of damaged proteins and organelles, promoted by energy stress and lower mTOR signaling. (Ch 5)

**Bonus Decade** — The later-life period of continued capability that the book contrasts with a prolonged Marginal Decade. (Ch 3)

**Calcium score** — Coronary-artery calcium measurement that records calcified plaque and refines, but does not settle, cardiovascular risk. (Ch 7)

**Centenarian Decathlon** — Ten or more future physical tasks used to define and train for desired late-life capacity. (Ch 11)

**CGM** — Continuous glucose monitor; a dynamic feedback tool for observing individual glucose responses. (Ch 2, 15)

**Cognitive reserve** — Capacity built through education, skills, and varied experience that can delay functional effects of brain pathology. (Ch 9)

**Compression of morbidity** — Delaying disease and disability so that the period of decline is shorter relative to total life. (Ch 4)

**CR** — Caloric restriction: reducing energy intake without malnutrition. (Ch 5, 15)

**DBT** — Dialectical Behavior Therapy, a skills-based approach emphasizing mindfulness, regulation, distress tolerance, relationships, and self-management. (Ch 17)

**Deep NREM sleep** — Slow-wave sleep associated with memory consolidation and cerebrospinal-fluid clearance of neural waste. (Ch 16)

**DR** — Dietary restriction: limiting a macronutrient, food category, or eating pattern. (Ch 15)

**Efficacy versus effectiveness** — What an intervention can do under ideal adherence versus what a prescription achieves in real life. (Ch 14)

**Evidence-informed medicine** — Using trial evidence while assessing how its population, protocol, and assumptions transfer to an individual. (Ch 2, 3)

**Four Horsemen** — Cardiovascular disease, cancer, neurodegenerative disease, and type 2 diabetes/metabolic dysfunction. (Ch 1)

**Healthspan** — Years of preserved physical, cognitive, and emotional function, not just years alive or free of coded disease. (Introduction, Ch 1)

**Hyperinsulinemia** — Persistently elevated insulin, often an early compensation for insulin resistance before glucose becomes diagnostic. (Ch 6)

**Insulin resistance** — Reduced tissue response to insulin, requiring more insulin to manage glucose and fuel traffic. (Ch 6, 16)

**Lp(a)** — Genetically variable LDL-like particle with an apo(a) component and atherogenic/thrombotic properties. (Ch 7)

**Marginal Decade** — Late-life period dominated by dependence or loss of capacity when reserve was not preserved. (Ch 3)

**Medicine 2.0** — Scientific modern medicine’s acute-treatment and evidence-based average model, highly successful against infection. (Ch 2)

**Medicine 3.0** — Proposed preventive, individualized, long-horizon, risk-aware, healthspan-centered medical mindset. (Ch 2)

**Metabolic syndrome** — Cluster including high blood pressure, triglycerides, low HDL, waist circumference, and fasting glucose; three or more criteria qualify in the book’s example. (Ch 6)

**mTOR** — Nutrient-sensitive growth pathway; high activity favors growth and synthesis, while lower activity favors maintenance and recycling. (Ch 5)

**NAFLD** — Nonalcoholic fatty liver disease, excess liver fat that can be silent and potentially reversible before advanced scarring. (Ch 6)

**NASH** — Nonalcoholic steatohepatitis: fatty liver with inflammation and possible fibrosis. (Ch 6)

**Nutrition 3.0** — Personalized nutritional biochemistry driven by physiology, feedback, outcomes, and sustainability rather than labels. (Ch 14)

**PREDIMED** — Large Spanish trial in which olive-oil- or nut-nudged Mediterranean patterns reduced major cardiovascular events in a high-risk population. (Ch 14)

**Risk × time** — Cardiovascular framing in which cumulative exposure can matter more than a short-term risk snapshot. (Ch 7)

**Sarcopenia** — Age-related loss of muscle mass and strength that reduces reserve and raises vulnerability. (Ch 11, 15)

**Sleep pressure** — Accumulated drive to sleep, influenced by wakefulness, caffeine, exercise, and naps. (Ch 16)

**Stability** — Coordinated ability to harness, transmit, and decelerate force through the body. (Ch 13)

**TR** — Time restriction: limiting the daily or periodic window for eating. (Ch 15)

**VO2 max** — Maximum rate of oxygen utilization; a strong fitness and longevity marker in the chapter’s cited cohorts. (Ch 11, 12)

**Zone 2** — Sustainable aerobic effort used to develop mitochondrial function, fuel flexibility, and aerobic efficiency. (Ch 12)
