# Glossary

**CBD (corticobasal degeneration)** — the rare, progressive brain disease diagnosed as the likely explanation for Moth's pain, tremor, facial numbness, and later decline; the memoir stresses diagnostic uncertainty and the absence of a treatment that reverses it (Ch 3).

**Coast Path / South West Coast Path** — the roughly 630-mile route from Minehead through the South West to South Haven Point, which becomes the memoir's physical itinerary and temporary home (Ch 1, Ch 3).

**Edgelanders** — Winn's name for people living between categories such as tame and wild, belonging and exile, life and death, or lost and found (Ch 15).

**Fudge diet** — an improvised, nutritionally poor response to dwindling money and food, sustained for stretches by cheap fudge bars and later elderberries; it marks scarcity rather than a recommended practice (Ch 7–8).

**Homeless hikers** — Moth's wry description of their position when they have no home but still have a route, equipment, and a purpose (Ch 19).

**Leave no trace** — the walkers' camping ethic: arrive late when needed, leave early, remove pegs and loose earth, and try to make the site look unused; the memoir shows both their learning and the limits imposed by fear and weather (Ch 4, Ch 19).

**Lightly salted blackberries** — the closing image for a fleeting, preserved, sunlit present: changed by the elements, still alive, and needing no guarantee beyond the moment (Ch 20).

**Moth** — Raynor Winn's partner and fellow walker, living with probable CBD while contributing humor, planning, storytelling, physical effort, and decisions throughout the journey (Ch 1–20).

**Paddy Dillon** — the author of the waterproof guidebook *The South West Coast Path: Minehead to South Haven Point*, carried in the walkers' pockets and used to orient each day (Ch 3, Ch 20).

**Pencarrow Head** — the Cornish headland associated with the farm, the walkers' return, and the ending of their homeless trail (Ch 16, Ch 20).

**Polruan** — the destination at the western end of their first walk and, later, the place where Anna offers them a small apartment; the route's end becomes the beginning of a possible home (Ch 20).

**Seismic shift** — Winn's description of the simultaneous collapse of home, income, legal security, and expected future, intensified by Moth's diagnosis (Ch 3).

**Smotyn** — the 19-year-old ewe who remains after the other livestock are sold; her death under the beech trees completes the emotional loss of the farm (Ch 2).

**Voluntary homelessness** — the difficult choice to leave a friend’s shed so that it can be rented, enabling the household to fund Moth's university start; it is a constrained decision, not proof that housing insecurity is freely chosen (Ch 17).

**Wild camping** — sleeping outside formal sites with minimal equipment, uncertain legality, and constant attention to shelter, water, food, tides, weather, and concealment (Ch 3–20).
