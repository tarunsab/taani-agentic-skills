# Glossary — 8 Rules of Love

**Agape** — Universal love in the Greek typology discussed in the Introduction. (Ch 1)

**Ashram** — A school or sanctuary for learning, growth, and support; the book’s map of life stages. (Ch 1)

**Brahmacharya** — The ashram of preparing for love through solitude and self-knowledge. (Ch 1–2)

**Dharma** — Purpose at the intersection of passion, skill, and service. (Ch 6)

**Emotional trust** — Confidence in a partner’s values, character, and treatment of people. (Ch 4)

**Eudaimonia** — Meaning- and purpose-based well-being, contrasted with pleasure-based hedonia. (Ch 6)

**Grhastha** — The householder ashram of practicing love with another while retaining the self. (Ch 1, 4)

**Guru** — A compassionate guide who supports growth without ownership or coercion. (Ch 5)

**Hedonia** — Satisfaction associated with pleasure, status, or personal gain. (Ch 6)

**Kama** — Pleasure and connection with others in the Vedic four-pursuit model. (Ch 6)

**Karma** — Cause and effect understood as feedback that teaches through consequences. (Ch 3, 8)

**Karuna** — Compassion for living entities. (Ch 9)

**Moksha** — Liberation from the material world and connection with spirit. (Ch 6)

**Opulences** — Knowledge, fame, money, beauty, strength, and renunciation; attractive signals that do not prove character. (Ch 3)

**PEACE** — Conflict sequence: Place and time, Expression, Anger management, Commitment, Evolution. (Ch 7)

**Preyas** — What we seek or want, contrasted with what we ought to seek. (Ch 9)

**Radius of respect** — A protective distance from which one can remain a well-wisher without inviting harm. (Ch 9)

**Samskara** — An absorbed impression from early experience, culture, media, or relationships that shapes later choices. (Ch 3)

**Sannyasa** — The ashram of extending love through service and compassion beyond the immediate self and family. (Ch 1, 9)

**Sat, chit, ananda** — The Vedic description of the soul as eternal, conscious/knowledge-filled, and blissful. (Ch 9)

**Self-expansion theory** — The idea that relationships enlarge the self through another person’s skills, traits, and perspectives. (Ch 5)

**Self-regulation** — Calming, supporting, or motivating oneself rather than outsourcing every emotional state. (Ch 2, 8)

**Śreyas** — What we ought to seek when we have the capacity to look beyond immediate needs. (Ch 9)

**Solitude** — Active, curious, self-directed time alone; distinct from loneliness. (Ch 2)

**Three-Date Rule** — A focusing lens for exploring personality, values, and goals, one dimension at a time. (Ch 4)

**Trust** — Gradual confidence built through congruent thoughts, words, actions, and repair. (Ch 4)

**Vanaprastha** — The healing ashram of reflection, conflict resolution, forgiveness, and letting go. (Ch 1, 8)
