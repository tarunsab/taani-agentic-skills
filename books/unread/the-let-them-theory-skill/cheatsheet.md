# Cheatsheet

## The two-step move

| If you notice… | Say / ask | Then choose… |
|---|---|---|
| Another adult is acting, thinking, or feeling outside your control | **Let Them** | Release the control attempt |
| You are calmer but still stuck | **Let Me** | Name your value, need, boundary, or next action |
| The issue is dangerous, discriminatory, abusive, or repeatedly harmful | Let Them is not the finish | Protect safety, speak up, report, set distance, or leave |

## Fast diagnostics

- Will this matter in an hour, a week, or a year? Release a passing irritation; act on a persistent or values-relevant issue.
- Are you trying to change a fixed trait/history? That is comparison torture: Let Them have their cards and stop self-punishment.
- Are you seeing a changeable result? Let Them lead the way; extract the formula and put in the reps.
- Are you waiting to be invited? Let Me go first; build weak ties and create the plan.
- Is a friend distant? Check proximity, timing, and energy before assuming rejection; assume good intent and do not keep score.

## Influence decision tree

1. Want an adult to change? Stop pressure and Let Them be.
2. Need a constructive attempt? Use the ABC Loop: apologize, ask open questions, back off, model, and celebrate progress.
3. Expecting them to change? Drop the expectation or resentment will replace influence.
4. Still unchanged after the book’s suggested time? Decide whether the issue is a deal breaker for you.

## Support versus rescue

| Support | Rescue / enabling |
|---|---|
| Safety intervention in a crisis | Shielding an adult from every consequence |
| Love, listening, practical help, and a healing environment | Money, excuses, or taking over without conditions |
| Clear conditions for financial help | Funding while treatment, work, or responsibility is refused |
| Standing beside someone as they face a hard thing | Doing the hard thing for them |

## Love rules

- In dating, watch behavior: mutual effort, respect, feelings, attraction, and interest.
- If someone will not commit, have one clear conversation focused on the value of your time; accept the answer.
- If a partner will not change, ask whether you can love them exactly as they are.
- If you cannot stop complaining and the issue threatens a dream or value, end the bitching or end the relationship.
- In heartbreak, let grief happen, reduce triggers/contact, fill the calendar, ask for support, and do the work for yourself rather than for an ex.
