# Cheatsheet

## Decision rules

| If you observe… | Ask / do… | Book anchor |
|---|---|---|
| A visible outcome depends on unseen protection | Map the decision-maker's blind side and trace the largest downside backward | Ch 1, 9 |
| A new threat makes the old playbook unsafe | Redesign adjacent roles, then test the new gap created by the fix | Ch 1, 5 |
| Low performance plus unstable basics | Check food, sleep, safety, transport, documents, and prior instruction before labeling capacity | Ch 3, 11 |
| A learner can demonstrate but not explain | Make the task spatial, embodied, and repeatable; verify in action | Ch 4, 8, 10 |
| A team has talent but too many decisions | Pre-decide the sequence and rehearse timing around the real bottleneck | Ch 5, 10 |
| A role is valuable but invisible | Measure avoided failures and counterfactual absence, not applause or raw production alone | Ch 2, 9 |
| Care overlaps with recruiting or money | Run a motive audit; separate necessary support from inducement | Ch 7, 8 |
| A serious mistake occurs during transition | Bridge safety, advice, accountability, and a return to routine | Ch 12 |
| One success is used as a universal template | Audit rare inputs and look for near-misses before scaling | Ch 11, 12, 13 |

## Quick distinctions

- **Visibility ≠ value:** a sack is visible; preventing it may be the more valuable act.
- **Potential ≠ readiness:** Michael's body and feet did not mean he knew Ole Miss's offense.
- **Access ≠ preparation:** admission without food, literacy, documents, or tutoring leaves the opportunity fragile.
- **Care ≠ inducement:** genuine support can coexist with institutional conflicts that still require scrutiny.
- **Motive ≠ outcome:** protective intent does not erase harm from uncontrolled action.
- **Price ≠ proof:** a salary reveals perceived scarcity and risk, not a perfect causal ranking.
- **Nurture ≠ guarantee:** a changed environment can reveal or develop capacity without making success inevitable.

## Smells and limits

- Blaming the person before inspecting the system.
- Adding complexity to a learner who has not mastered the spatial base case.
- Using “natural ability” to skip teaching, or “learning disability” to stop looking for capacity.
- Treating one family, school, or coach as a scalable program without naming its rare resources.
- Using a market prototype as a universal definition of talent.
- Treating an exceptional outcome as independent proof of the book's historical or statistical claims.

## Minimal application loop

1. Name the visible outcome and the hidden exposure.
2. Identify the constraint, missing prerequisite, or costly failure.
3. Redesign the system or support around that constraint.
4. Demonstrate, rehearse, and verify in the real channel.
5. Audit trade-offs, motives, and who remains outside the pathway.
