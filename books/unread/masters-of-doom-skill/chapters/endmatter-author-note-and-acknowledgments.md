# End Matter: Author Note and Acknowledgments

## Core Idea

The extracted source ends with an unlabeled author reflection followed by acknowledgments rather than another numbered chapter. David Kushner describes his own route into gaming and the experience of discovering the Doom and Quake subculture, then explains that years of reporting were intended to understand the people and culture rather than reproduce a simple triumph story. The note positions the book as reported narrative shaped by access to interviewees, archival material, and the author’s perspective as a gamer. The acknowledgments identify the Two Johns, interviewees, editors, agent, publisher, and family as part of the work’s production. This matters for using the skill because the chapters are a synthesis of a particular author’s evidence and framing, not an omniscient technical history.

## Frameworks Introduced

The end matter supplies a provenance principle: interpret a narrative knowledge base in light of who gathered the evidence and what they were trying to understand. It also makes collaboration visible; a finished book is an authored artifact supported by sources, editors, institutions, and family labor. Use this note when distinguishing source-grounded reporting from independently verified claims or from the skill’s own abstractions.

## Key Concepts

- **Authorial position** — Kushner writes as a gamer who entered and studied the culture he describes.
- **Reported synthesis** — the book combines interviews, research, and narrative interpretation.
- **Acknowledgments** — the production network behind the book is part of its provenance.

## Mental Models

Treat the book as a lens with a visible maker, not as a neutral database. That does not invalidate the reported examples; it tells the reader to preserve distinctions among observed events, attributed statements, and the author’s interpretation. The same discipline should guide any skill generated from a narrative source.

## Anti-patterns

- Presenting the skill’s abstractions as if they were independent empirical laws.
- Removing the author’s perspective and thereby hiding selection, emphasis, and uncertainty.
- Treating acknowledgments as decorative instead of evidence of collaborative production.

## Worked Example

The author’s note changes how a reader should use the entire skill. A statement about the Hacker Ethic, the founders’ motives, or the meaning of a fan community belongs first to Kushner’s reported narrative. A framework such as “small-team ceiling” is then a careful synthesis across those episodes, not a controlled experiment. Keeping both layers visible makes the notes more useful: they can support study and application without pretending to offer more certainty than the source provides.

## Key Takeaways

- Provenance and authorial perspective belong in a complete conversion.
- Synthesis should remain distinguishable from source event and quotation.
- Collaboration is part of the history of the book as well as the history it tells.
