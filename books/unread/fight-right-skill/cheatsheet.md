# Fight Right Cheatsheet

## First, choose the right goal

| If you notice | Goal |
|---|---|
| A one-off logistical problem | Find a practical solution. |
| A recurring difference in values, personality, or lifestyle | Understand, manage, and revisit it. |
| Repetition, hurt, polarization, and no progress | Move from gridlock to dialogue; uncover the dream. |
| A recent or old incident still hurts | Process the single incident after both are calm. |
| Coercion, characterological violence, addiction refusal, or unsafe participation | Seek specialized help/protection; do not run a couple exercise as if this were ordinary conflict. |

## End-to-end sequence

1. **Start right** — “I feel [emotion] about [situation], and I need [positive need].” One issue only; no “you always/never,” blame, criticism, contempt, or kitchen sinking.
2. **Listen** — Postpone your case. Ask questions. Summarize to your partner’s satisfaction. Validate: “I understand why you’d feel that way.” Validation is not agreement.
3. **Stay collaborative** — If flooded, say: “We need a break. I’m going to [place/activity] and will return at [time].” Leave visual range; soothe, do not rehearse rebuttals. Take at least 20 minutes; return within 24 hours or renegotiate clearly.
4. **Solve the moment** — Do not aim to win, persuade, or finish a perpetual problem. Use self-description, a clear need, and small repairs: apology, empathy, appreciation, humor that is not sarcastic, affection if welcome, or “I see your point.” Keep positivity ahead of negativity (5:1 in conflict; 20:1 outside it).
5. **Get to the dream** — For a repeating or disproportionate fight, use speaker/listener roles and ask: beliefs/values? history? importance? feelings? ideal dream? purpose/goal? Do not persuade or compromise yet.
6. **Find flexibility** — Inner bagel = nonnegotiable core. Outer ring = flexible when/where/how much/how often/sequence/duration. Ask: What do we agree on? What feelings/shared goals? What plan honors both cores? Revisit temporary plans.
7. **Process the past** — Only from the balcony: (1) feelings, not why; (2) each reality, then summarize/validate; (3) triggers and earlier stories; (4) responsibility and specific apology; (5) one constructive change each. No fact trial, intent assignment, or piling on other incidents.

## Fast tells

- **Harsh start** → expect defend; restart with self + situation + positive need.
- **Heart racing, heat, tightness, spinning mind** → stop action; flooding means skill access is offline.
- **The same “solution” keeps failing** → look beneath the surface for a dream or value.
- **Every “yes” feels like losing** → ask what is core versus flexible; accept influence.
- **A good apology does not land** → partner may still need impact, trigger, or trust addressed.
- **A neutral act feels hostile** → check the negative perspective and the emotion bank account.
