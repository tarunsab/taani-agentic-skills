# Cheatsheet

These are decision aids distilled from the book, not current clinical protocols.

## First-pass rule

| If the situation is… | Start with… | Escalate when… |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary maintenance | Varied food, fiber, movement, sleep, sensible fluids, no tobacco, moderated alcohol | The change persists or function declines |
| A mild, familiar nuisance | A low-risk mechanism-matched adjustment | It is severe, recurrent, or not improving |
| Sudden or dangerous | Emergency services/current medical care | Do not delay for a home hack |

## High-value defaults

- Prefer dietary patterns over “superfoods”: plants, wholegrains, beans, nuts, seeds, fermented foods, and suitable omega-3 sources.
- Hydrate steadily; avoid both dehydration and rapid overconsumption.
- Aim toward the book's activity reference of 150 minutes of moderate activity weekly plus strength work.
- Protect sleep timing with morning light, evening wind-down, and consistent wake time.
- Avoid smoking/vaping; cut down or avoid excess alcohol.
- Use sunscreen year-round; SPF 30 blocks about 97% of UVB in the book's example.

## Fast body checks

| Notice | Book's interpretation/action |
|---|---|
| Stool 1–2 on Bristol scale | Often constipation; consider fluid/fiber, but persistent change needs review |
| Stool 3–4 | Typical target range |
| Stool 5–7 | Diarrhea pattern; persistence or systemic symptoms need review |
| Clay/gray stool, black stool, or fresh blood | Possible obstruction or bleeding; seek medical assessment |
| Sudden floaters plus flashes | Urgent eye assessment |
| Burning urine plus fever/vomiting/shivers | Possible ascending infection; seek prompt care |
| Severe testicular pain or abnormal position | Time-critical emergency |
| Chest pain/shortness of breath/collapse | Emergency assessment; do not assume “heart attack” and “cardiac arrest” are identical |

## Mechanism-matched hacks

- Screen strain → 20/20/20 breaks and distance viewing.
- Difficult stool → knees higher than hips; avoid straining or long toilet sessions.
- Motion sickness → look at the horizon and reduce visual/vestibular mismatch.
- Nosebleed → sit upright, lean slightly forward, pinch the soft nose.
- Ear pressure → yawn or chew to open the eustachian tubes; do not push Q-tips inward.
- Chilli heat → use dairy rather than water to dissolve capsaicin.
- Morning grogginess → water, natural light, a small food source if wanted, and gentle movement before phone stress.
- Stress surge → slow breathing; cold-face stimulation is described as a reset, not a cure.

## Thresholds the book emphasizes

- About 2 L fluid over a day as an introduction baseline; context matters.
- About 30 g fiber daily.
- No more than 5–10 minutes lingering on the toilet; avoid straining.
- 60 minutes of audio at about 60% volume, then a 15-minute break.
- Last caffeine around 2 P.M. if sleep is affected.
- A 20-minute nap or a complete 90-minute cycle; avoid late naps when they impair sleep drive.
- Testicular torsion: roughly six hours to restore blood flow before tissue loss risk rises.
