# Cheatsheet

Compact diagnostic rules from *The Scam*.

## Start with the transaction

| Ask | Evidence to reconcile |
|---|---|
| What was promised? | Contract, BR, SGL, allotment, or order |
| What actually moved? | Cash statement, security register, PDO/depository record |
| Who controlled it? | Legal owner, beneficial owner, common directors/accounts |
| Who carried the downside? | Bank, broker, fund, issuer, depositor, or investor |
| Who could stop it? | Desk, board, exchange, RBI/SEBI, auditor, court |

## Five fast tests

1. **Document test:** if a BR or certificate replaces delivery, independently verify the underlying asset.
2. **Capacity test:** compare a counterparty's capital and exposure; Metro's tiny capital against hundreds of crores is a warning.
3. **Funding test:** trace same-day transfers and calculate implied interest hidden in price or brokerage.
4. **Exit test:** ask who must buy the inventory if the operator stops supporting the price.
5. **Fiduciary test:** compare a bailout or trade with the duty owed to depositors, unit-holders, or clients.

## Read a price surge

Separate four variables: price, independent volume, ownership concentration, and funding source. Rising price plus circular volume and shared funding is a network position, not proof of broad demand. If institutions arrive only after the operator manufactures liquidity, their presence may be the exit leg.

## Read a regulator

Use the signal-to-action chain: warning captured → corroborated → owner assigned → intervention chosen → evidence preserved → result verified. A circular, penalty, ban, merger, or press release is not closure by itself. Ask whether the action recovered money, stopped the mechanism, and disclosed the decision-makers.

## Compare 1992 and 2001

| Dimension | 1992 | 2001 |
|---|---|---|
| Visible operator | Harshad Mehta | Ketan Parekh |
| Discovery hole | SBI securities/cash mismatch | Bank of India pay orders/MMCB |
| Core funding | Banks, PSUs, PMS, foreign banks | Corporate money, GTB, MMCB, FIIs, mutual funds |
| Market channel | Ready forwards, BRs, SGLs | K-10 stocks, cross-bourse trades, OCBs, synchronized orders |
| Failure signal | Missing securities and bank reconciliation | Dishonoured pay orders and collapsing inventory |

## Keep claims calibrated

Use “the book reports,” “SEBI found,” “the authors allege,” “a witness admitted,” or “a court convicted” as appropriate. Do not convert a relationship into proof, an agency assertion into a court finding, or a market outcome into a current investment rule.
