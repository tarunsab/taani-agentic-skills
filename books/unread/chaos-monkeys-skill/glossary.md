# Chaos Monkeys — Glossary

Compact definitions follow the book's historical usage; they are not current
legal, tax, privacy, or financial advice.

- **Ad server:** System that delivers ads and records the accounting facts of
  who saw what, when, where, and how often.
- **AdWords:** Google's keyword advertising auction, where bids and expected
  click quality influence placement.
- **Acqui-hire:** Acquisition structured primarily to hire a startup's people,
  often with separate retention offers.
- **Ads API:** Interface through which external software can manage or interact
  with a platform's advertising inventory.
- **Attribution:** Connecting an ad exposure or click to a later action such as
  a purchase; attribution is not automatically causal.
- **Bid-ask spread:** Difference between a market intermediary's buying and
  selling prices.
- **Brand advertising:** Spend intended to influence awareness or future demand,
  usually harder to tie to an immediate action.
- **Cap table:** Ownership ledger showing founders, investors, options, and
  dilution.
- **Capped convertible note:** Debt that converts to equity later, with a
  maximum conversion valuation that protects the early investor.
- **Clickthrough:** Immediate action after an ad, commonly a click.
- **Conversion tracking:** Measurement linking ad spend to a downstream business
  outcome.
- **Custom Audiences:** Facebook product for matching advertiser-provided
  identifiers to platform users for audience targeting.
- **Data onboarding:** Joining offline or CRM identifiers to online users or
  devices.
- **Demand-side platform (DSP):** Software that buys ad impressions for
  advertisers and agencies across exchanges.
- **Derivative:** Financial instrument whose value derives from an underlying
  asset, rate, or obligation.
- **Direct response:** Advertising optimized for a measurable action such as a
  click, lead, install, or purchase.
- **Dilution:** Reduction in an owner's percentage when new equity is issued.
- **DSP bid request:** Real-time message describing an impression opportunity so
  a buying system can decide whether and how much to bid.
- **Facebook Audience Network:** Facebook ad delivery powered by Facebook data
  but shown in other apps, as described in the epilogue.
- **Facebook Exchange (FBX):** Real-time exchange connecting outside ad-tech
  bidders to Facebook inventory in the historical account.
- **First-party data:** Information collected through a direct relationship with
  a user or customer.
- **Firm commitment:** Underwriting arrangement in which a bank buys an offering
  and assumes placement risk.
- **Fungibility:** Interchangeability of units, which makes standardized assets
  easier to trade and automate.
- **Growth team:** Facebook group focused on turning resources and campaigns into
  more users and engagement.
- **Identity graph:** Network of identifiers and evidence linking devices,
  records, events, and possibly people or households.
- **Incremental revenue:** New spend or value that would not have appeared
  without the product, rather than revenue reclassified from an old bucket.
- **IPO:** Initial public offering of company shares into public markets.
- **Lockup:** Period restricting when certain insiders can sell shares after an
  offering.
- **MMA ad unit:** Standard mobile advertising format associated with the Mobile
  Marketing Association.
- **Native ad format:** Platform-specific creative and delivery format that does
  not use standard display units.
- **No-shop clause:** Contract term restricting a seller from soliciting or
  negotiating with alternative buyers.
- **Product-market fit:** Evidence that a defined product solves a real problem
  for enough users at sustainable economics.
- **Programmatic advertising:** Automated buying and selling of ad impressions
  through systems and exchanges.
- **Publisher:** Site or app that provides content, users, and ad inventory.
- **Quality score:** Estimate of expected ad performance used in an auction;
  exact formulas vary by platform.
- **Retargeting:** Showing ads based on a user's prior browsing, product, or
  purchase behavior.
- **Restricted stock:** Equity subject to vesting and other transfer or tax
  conditions.
- **ROAS:** Return on ad spend, commonly `(revenue / cost) - 1` in the book's
  example.
- **Secondary market:** Market where existing private shares are sold rather
  than newly issued by the company.
- **Social graph:** Network of relationships and interactions among users,
  represented as platform data.
- **Sponsored Stories:** Facebook ad experiment that used social context around
  users' actions to promote content.
- **Term sheet:** Preliminary document describing economic and legal terms of a
  proposed financing or acquisition.
- **Third-party data:** Information collected or sold by an entity without the
  direct user relationship that generated the original activity.
- **Trough of Despair:** YC shorthand for the post-launch period when initial
  attention fades before product-market fit.
- **Valuation cap:** Maximum company valuation used to convert a capped note.
- **Vesting:** Gradual earning of equity or other compensation over time or on
  specified milestones.
- **Viewthrough:** Exposure to an ad followed by a later action without an
  immediate click.
- **Viral distribution:** Growth driven by sharing or external attention rather
  than a fully controlled paid-acquisition channel.
