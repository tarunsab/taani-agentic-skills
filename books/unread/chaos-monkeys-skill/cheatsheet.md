## Measurement checks

- **ROAS example:** `(revenue / cost) - 1`; pair it with margin, churn, and
  acquisition cost.
- **Incrementality:** compare with the counterfactual, not the dashboard bucket.
- **Scale:** report both percentage lift and absolute dollars or users.
- **Attribution:** separate exposure, click, conversion, and causal lift.
- **Identity:** record source, confidence, permission, retention, and deletion.
- **Latency:** sum network, lookup, bid, auction, and rendering time; fail safely.

## Financing and exit checks

1. Model valuation cap, discount, option pool, dilution, and later rounds.
2. Before exclusivity, preserve credible alternatives and request a complete term sheet.
3. Build a payout waterfall: company, investors, founders, employees, tax, escrow.
4. Treat vesting, lockup, and termination as economic terms.
5. Treat the acquisition as a new job: manager, role, autonomy, IP, and restrictions.

## Culture and execution checks

- Convert each value into a ritual, tool, metric, or consequence.
- Give experiments an owner, baseline, success threshold, stop date, and blast radius.
- Protect builders from noise without hiding material risks.
- Use layered safety: model, human review, user signal, appeal, and audit.
- Run a mortality review: what could replace the core product, and what evidence would reveal it?

## Core warnings

- Viral attention is not retention.
- Intimate data is not automatically useful data.
- A first-day IPO pop may be underpricing.
- A high nominal grant is not realized wealth.
- A phone call or signed term sheet is stronger evidence than social enthusiasm.
- The successful retrospective narrative may be the least reproducible part of the story.
