# Glossary

- **A-12 / Oxcart:** CIA reconnaissance aircraft that evolved into the Mach 3 Blackbird family.
- **Airframe:** The aircraft structure excluding propulsion and many installed systems.
- **Blackbird:** Family including the A-12, YF-12, M-21/D-21 carrier combination, and SR-71; the memoir emphasizes Mach 3 reconnaissance.
- **D-21 / Tagboard:** High-speed, low-observable reconnaissance drone, first carried by a Blackbird and later launched from a B-52.
- **Echo I:** Denys Overholser and Bill Schroeder's computer method for estimating radar return from faceted shapes.
- **F-117A:** Operational stealth attack aircraft developed from Have Blue.
- **Have Blue / XST:** Lockheed's stealth demonstrator and prototype program that validated low radar observability in flight.
- **Habu:** Okinawan name applied by crews to the Blackbird because its shape suggested a venomous snake.
- **Hopeless Diamond:** Informal name for the faceted stealth concept that became the basis of Have Blue.
- **Inlet unstart:** Loss of stable airflow at a supersonic engine inlet; on the Blackbird it could cause a violent jolt and major thrust loss.
- **Low observability / stealth:** Deliberate reduction of a platform's detectability across relevant signatures, especially radar in this narrative.
- **Need to know:** Access rule limiting sensitive program information to people whose work requires it.
- **Pogo:** Temporary ground support used under the U-2's lightweight, bicycle-style landing arrangement.
- **Radar cross section (RCS):** Effective measure of how strongly a target reflects radar energy back toward a receiver.
- **Silver bullet:** Rich's metaphor for a small number of highly capable, highly protected weapons used against decisive targets.
- **Skunk Works:** Lockheed's Advanced Development Projects organization, presented as a small, independent, high-speed development unit.
- **SWATH:** Small-Waterplane-Area Twin Hull; Sea Shadow's catamaran-like geometry that reduces wave-making and supports low detectability.
- **Suntan / CL-400:** Hydrogen-fueled high-altitude aircraft study that failed its range target but produced useful technology.
- **Tailpipe:** Exhaust section behind a jet engine; on the F-117 it was a difficult heat and infrared-management problem.
- **Tagboard:** See D-21.
- **Total Quality Management:** The memoir's quality-at-source analogue: workers verify work, independent inspection can stop it, and defects are found early.
- **U-2:** High-altitude reconnaissance aircraft designed under Kelly Johnson for CIA-directed intelligence collection.
- **Unstable aircraft:** An aircraft whose natural motion diverges without continuous computerized control; Have Blue and F-117 relied on fly-by-wire stabilization.
