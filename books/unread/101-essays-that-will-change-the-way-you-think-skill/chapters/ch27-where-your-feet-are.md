# Essay 27: Be Where Your Feet Are: Mantras That Will Remind You That Your Life Is Happening in This Moment

## Core Idea

Presence is the only place life can be lived, yet attention is continually claimed by imagined futures, unfinished potential, and external demands. Wiest argues that every dream, relationship, and goal grows out of the present moment, so what one does now is not a small preliminary action but the material of the whole life. The mantras are short prompts that move attention from abstraction to today’s ordinary choices, available resources, priorities, and bodily presence. They do not promise that repeating a phrase solves resistance; they function as re-grounding cues. The governing instruction is to be where your feet are and to recognize “I am here.”

## The 15 Mantras

1. Attend to what is directly in front of you.
2. Remember that unrealized potential becomes painful.
3. Make the ordinary the site of extraordinary action.
4. Work little by little.
5. Remember that now is all you have.
6. Ask what your desired life would make today look like.
7. Ask what your desired love would make today look like.
8. Start where you are with what you have.
9. Identify what most matters today.
10. Treat your life as the sum of your days.
11. Ask what your realized self would do today.
12. Check whether you are doing your best right now.
13. Ask what you would do if tiredness were not deciding.
14. Be where your feet are.
15. Say: I am here.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Present-moment cueing**
  - When to use: Attention has drifted into future fantasy, regret, or performance.
  - How: Repeat a short question or mantra, then choose the next action visible in the present.
- **Ordinary-to-extraordinary translation**
  - When to use: You are waiting for ideal conditions to begin.
  - How: Ask what the desired life would look like in this ordinary day and do one piece of it.

## Key Concepts

- **Presence**: Direct contact with what is happening now.
- **Mantra**: A brief phrase used to re-ground attention and choice.
- **Unrealized potential**: The pain of ideas that remain disconnected from action.
- **Ordinary action**: The daily material through which an extraordinary life is built.

## Mental Models

- Translate a five-year wish into the smallest version that fits today.
- If the mind is everywhere, bring it back to feet, breath, and the next task.
- Use “I am here” as a fact, not a performance of calm.

## Anti-patterns

- **Using future fantasy as a substitute for action**: Potential does not become a life until expressed today.
- **Waiting for a different day**: The desired life has to be practiced in the current conditions.

## Key Takeaways

1. Bring every large desire back to today.
2. Use the mantra that interrupts your particular form of drift.
3. Let small action make potential real.
4. Return to physical presence when the mind leaves the moment.
