# Cheatsheet

Use these questions as a quick diagnostic when applying the book’s frameworks.

| Decision | Evidence to check | Warning sign | Safer rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enter a regulated city | Authorization path, stop conditions, local owner | “Launch first; settle later” | Pair growth experiments with a compliance decision owner and exit trigger. |
| Seed a marketplace | Liquidity, retention, subsidy dependence, fraud | Growth rises only while bonuses rise | Define the subsidy’s purpose and the metric that ends it. |
| Remove product friction | Which step disappeared and who performed it? | Safety, consent, or pay becomes invisible | Keep friction that functions as a control; explain the rest. |
| Choose founder-control terms | Voting, board, information, and removal rights | Economics are shared but decisions are not | Preserve a credible intervention path and independent oversight. |
| Use sensitive data | Purpose, consent, access, retention, auditability | Different service for regulators or rivals | Separate protection from competition; require review before launch. |
| Handle a misconduct report | Recurrence, retaliation risk, manager status | High performer gets a “first offense” exception | Investigate independently; protect the reporter, not the hierarchy. |
| Respond to a public backlash | Context, prior trust, affected stakeholders | Technical defense ignores symbolic harm | Acknowledge impact, change the practice, and show evidence. |
| Manage a founder exit | Access, board seats, allies, communications | Former CEO still directs employees | Make operational separation explicit before succession. |
| Repair after crisis | Governance, CFO/legal/security authority, worker feedback | New values but old decision rights | Change the system, then rebuild the story. |

## Fast tells

- **“Super pumped”** can mean useful urgency; ask what prevents exhaustion, retaliation, or unsafe shortcuts.
- **“Founder friendly”** can mean trust; ask who can remove the founder if trust fails.
- **“Customer obsession”** can mean service quality; ask whether drivers, workers, and communities are visible in the metric.
- **“Security”** can mean protection; ask whether the same access is being used to surveil, compete, or evade oversight.
- **“Independent investigation”** is credible only when its methods, investigators, and implementation are independent.

## Minimal application loop

1. Map the stakeholders and the hidden work behind the visible product.
2. Separate growth signals from durable economics and legitimacy.
3. Identify who has information, voting power, and authority to stop the system.
4. Add the missing control before adding more speed.
