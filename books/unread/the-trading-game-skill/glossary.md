# Glossary

**Book** — The inventory of positions for which a trader has pricing or risk ownership; owning a book is the route to direct PnL in the narrative (Ch 4, Ch 7).

**Central bank** — The institution that sets or steers short-term interest rates and can intervene in funding markets; the Fed, ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Japan, and SNB shape the book's trade logic (Ch 4, Ch 10).

**Cover trade** — A junior's execution and risk-management task: check a price, quote, book a swap, then hold, hedge, or work the position for a margin (Ch 4).

**CYA / Cover Your Arse** — Bill's rule to keep one's actions defensible and leave no unexplained trail that an opponent can use; it is a survival lesson, not an ethical defense of wrongdoing (Ch 5).

**Deferred compensation** — Bonus money paid in later years and therefore usable by the bank as a retention mechanism; it becomes Gary's financial handcuff in Tokyo (Ch 12, Ch 15).

**FX swap** — A collateralized, two-way currency loan: borrow one currency and lend another, with the interest-rate differential determining the price (Ch 4).

**Hoot** — A floor-wide audio channel used for announcements, jokes, and Caleb's morning explanation of LIBOR and the banking crisis (Ch 4).

**LIBOR** — The interbank funding benchmark whose rise signals that banks distrust one another; in the memoir, widening funding spreads drive STIRT profitability (Ch 4).

**OIS / Eurodollar** — Short-term interest-rate products used for bets on future policy rates and dollar funding; Gary uses them to express his recovery and inequality thesis (Ch 10, Ch 12).

**PnL** — Profit and loss, the daily and cumulative measure of what a trader makes for the bank and the main input to status and bonus conversations (Ch 4, Ch 6).

**Position** — The complete list of a trader's outstanding trades, including currency, date, size, and direction; Spengler's spreadsheet makes a position legible (Ch 5).

**Spread** — The gap between a price at which a dealer buys and sells; wider spreads transfer more margin to the owner of the relevant book (Ch 3, Ch 4).

**STIRT** — The short-term interest-rate trading desk that handles FX swaps and related money-market products; the book's main institutional setting (Ch 4).

**Yard** — Desk slang for one billion dollars, used to make enormous positions sound ordinary (Ch 7).
