# Cheatsheet

## Before acting on a market view

| Question | Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| What is mispriced? | Name the price, spread, or consensus—not just the story. |
| Why is it wrong? | Point to a mechanism or observable evidence. |
| Who is on the other side? | Identify their information, constraints, and incentive. |
| What breaks the thesis? | Write the causal failure chain. |
| Can I survive it? | Size for a bad case and a worse case; timing is risk. |

## Warning signs

- Everyone is doing the same trade and calling it free money.
- Senior approval is being used as proof of safety.
- The PnL is visible, but the risk owner and tail scenario are vague.
- A position looks diversified but its legs depend on the same funding or macro event.
- The market's “representative” person does not resemble the people actually carrying the debt.
- Compensation is delayed until leaving becomes financially or psychologically impossible.
- Belonging, alcohol, status, or fear is substituting for independent judgment.

## Exit checklist

1. Map deferred pay, vesting, notice, sick-leave, and lawful release routes.
2. Get independent medical, legal, and financial advice where relevant.
3. Keep records of decisions and preserve external relationships.
4. Set a health boundary that the institution cannot redefine.
5. Decide what you are willing to lose before the negotiation begins.

## Recurring distinctions

| Distinction | Keep separate |
|---|---|
| Right vs. profitable | A correct view can lose before it pays. |
| Popular vs. safe | Participation can be correlated risk. |
| Salary vs. PnL | The bank's earnings are not the trader's freedom. |
| Status vs. belonging | Approval is not durable support. |
| Wealth vs. wellbeing | More money can coexist with less agency. |
